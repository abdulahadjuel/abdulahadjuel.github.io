EE 617 · Sequences and Codes in DSP Applications

Digital signal processing meets machine learning

What I learned

A communication system is a chain: data generation, encoding, modulation, a noisy channel, then evaluation. BER measures reliability as SNR changes, and a genetic algorithm can search for better sequences when the fitness function weighs both BER and sequence correlation.

So what

BER was about 0.021 for random sequences, 0.042 for Walsh and 0.010 for the GA-optimized sequence. GA plus coding reached 0.031, helping at some low SNR values and degrading at intermediate ones. One performance number is never enough.

Now what

I want to combine machine learning with traditional engineering methods, and test adaptive, SNR-aware strategies on more realistic channels. The same thinking applies to smart grids and renewable energy systems.

EE 559 · Special Topics: Optimization and AI for Next-Gen Smart Grid

Reading infeasibility as information

What I learned

When demand exceeds what the network can supply, Newton–Raphson load flow can diverge because no feasible operating point exists. Adding mismatch variables to a softened Kirchhoff's Current Law makes the infeasibility measurable, and an L1 penalty keeps only a few of them nonzero.

So what

On the IEEE 30-bus case, the dense formulation was solved at a load factor of 4.3 and the sparse one at 9.5. The sparse result points to a few critical locations, which links the math to decisions such as generation expansion or load shedding.

Now what

I plan to apply sparse formulations to renewable-energy integration, stability and energy management, and to pair them with data-driven forecasts that flag stressed conditions early.

EE 590 · Consumer Optoelectronics

Systems, users and the thermal camera

What I learned

Understanding a device means understanding how its parts work together and how clearly it presents information to the person using it. Explaining why a design choice matters taught me more than naming components.

So what

The thermal camera project showed me the value of starting with the purpose: what information is needed and how each part contributes. Clear presentation and reliability matter alongside technical performance.

Now what

I want to keep exploring sensing and intelligent electronic systems, especially measurements that support monitoring and help identify unusual conditions.

EE 530 · Fundamentals of Semiconductor Devices

From equations to physical meaning

What I learned

Using the Bohr model, I studied how an electron's potential energy varies with its distance from the nucleus, and how force follows the negative gradient of that energy. Reading the slope of the curve, not only its height, is what makes the connection.

So what

Setting a diagram beside its equation lets me ask what changes and what stays fixed. Recognizing a formula is not the same as understanding it, so I now restate each idea in plain words.

Now what

I will keep linking equations, diagrams and physical meaning as I move into electronic and optoelectronic systems, and keep practicing explaining technical ideas clearly to others.

EE 559 · Optimization and AI for Next-Gen Smart Grid

Augmented power flow analysis via sparse optimization

When a stressed network has no feasible power-flow solution, this method adds compensation variables to the balance equations and minimizes them. The dense version spreads the mismatch across the network. The sparse version, using an L1 penalty, concentrates it on a few buses.

  • Power flow
  • KCL relaxation
  • L1 sparsity
  • IEEE 30-bus

EE 617 · Sequences and Codes in DSP Applications

Genetic-algorithm sequence design with error-correcting codes

A Python and Jupyter simulation of random bits, block codes (BCH and LDPC), an AWGN channel and a genetic algorithm that searches for sequences with low BER and low correlation. Random and Walsh sequences set the baseline.

Reported BER: random 0.021, Walsh 0.042, GA 0.010, GA plus coding 0.031. The interactive chart on the Home page shows these values.

  • Genetic algorithm
  • BCH / LDPC
  • AWGN
